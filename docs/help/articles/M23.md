## P03 restore staging validation candidate (not yet an enabled workflow)

The native lexical staging validator shares the portable name policy with managed attachments. It does not create or restore a file, prove path ownership, or encrypt a backup. Actual restore remains gated on authenticated archive verification, safe staging, and independent integrity and recovery tests; preserve the original data.

### Create an encrypted portable backup (development candidate)
Open Backup Center and the portable chat-vault panel. Choose a strong passphrase (at least 12 UTF-8 bytes), confirm it and explicitly choose the local portable folder. Create encrypted backup. The archive includes saved vault records, committed managed files and published conversation exports within a 64 MiB payload limit. It excludes credentials, models, logs, caches, unlinked and staged files and previous backups. Copy the .awbackup to separate storage. Lost passphrases cannot be recovered. To verify and restore, place an archive in the local portable folder, enter its ID and passphrase, and consent to a separate plaintext profile. No active data is replaced. A verified marker is written only after authentication, integrity and linked-file validation. Interrupted stages are not verified profiles. The fixed destination is not a Save As picker. Automatic activation, retention, selective merge and full M23 operations remain unavailable. Candidate awaits native CI and security review.
