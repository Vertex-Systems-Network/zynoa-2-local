## Summary

Unified projects, recent work, filesystem scopes and per-project state.

## Getting started

The P01 GUI has no full Workspace Manager. Use **Overview** (`Ctrl+1`) to inspect its read-only native handshake, then **Recovery** (`Ctrl+3`) to see the no-backup placeholder. The **Create workspace** control is disabled; the options below are future requirements, not executable steps.

## Common workflows

- Confirm the read-only workspace handshake state on Overview.
- Check that **Create workspace** remains disabled.
- Review Recovery's no-backup placeholder without claiming restore support.
